What You Actually Get for the Price

The totes are industrial-strength plastic, the same bins used by commercial movers. Each one holds up to 60 lbs, stacks flat with identical lids, and doesn't need tape. Lid open. Stuff in. Lid shut. Stack.

The dolly matters more than people expect. A stack of 10 totes weighs 400–600 lbs. Moving that without a dolly is a bad time. It's included because it should be.

The 14-day window is intentional. We drop off 7 days before your move date so you have a full week to pack at a normal pace. After move day, you have another full week to unpack before we come collect. No rushing the unpack at midnight.

How It Compares to Buying Cardboard Boxes

Most people underestimate what cardboard costs. You're not just buying boxes; you're buying tape, markers, mattress bags, and then figuring out what to do with 40 flattened boxes at your new place. Brampton doesn't pick up cardboard in bulk on most collection days.

Here's a realistic cost for a 2-bedroom move using cardboard:

Item Cardboard Route ToteMove
Boxes / totes ~$120 (45 boxes, mixed sizes) Included in $189
Packing tape (3–4 rolls) ~$20 $0
Dolly rental (2 days) ~$40–$60 $0
Disposal / junk removal ~$40–$80 $0
Total ~$220–$280 $189

Cardboard usually costs more once you add it all up. And you still have to deal with the mess at the end.

Extended Rentals: What They Cost

Sometimes moves run long. Possession dates shift. The unpack takes more time than planned. That's fine.

Extended rentals are available at $1.50 per tote per week beyond the standard 14-day window. So if you have 30 totes and need an extra week, that's $45. Volume discounts apply for larger packages; contact us and we'll sort it out.

There's no penalty for asking. We'd rather you take the time you need than rush a move.

Is Tote Rental Worth It for a Small Move?

For a studio or 1-bedroom, the $119 price point is hard to argue with. Twenty totes is enough for most one-bedroom apartments, and you're getting a dolly and two-way delivery included. Buying 20 decent boxes plus tape and a dolly rental gets you to roughly the same number, without the convenience of someone dropping it at your door.

Where tote rental really pays off is the time you don't spend assembling, taping, breaking down, and disposing of cardboard. That's easy to dismiss until it's 9pm on move day and you're surrounded by flattened boxes.
