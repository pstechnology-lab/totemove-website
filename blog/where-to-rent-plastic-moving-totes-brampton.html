A note on terminology

Plastic moving totes, moving bins, reusable moving boxes, plastic moving boxes: these terms get used for the same thing. A rigid plastic container, roughly 24 × 20 × 12 inches, with a lid that snaps shut without tape. Holds around 60 lbs. Stacks cleanly in a truck without collapsing or absorbing moisture.

The practical difference from cardboard is that the walls don't flex or fail under load. A 60-lb tote at the bottom of a 4-high stack in a moving truck stays intact. A "large" cardboard box in the same situation is a gamble.

ToteMove's totes are heavy-duty polyethylene and get inspected between each rental. You can see the full cost comparison in the plastic tote rental cost breakdown.
