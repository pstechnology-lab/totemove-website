How It Works

Three easy steps: you focus on the move, we handle the rest.

1

We Deliver

Choose your package and move date. We'll drop off a stack of clean, sturdy totes and a dolly right at your door, the day before your move.

→
2

You Move

Pack at your own pace. Roll fully-loaded totes with your dolly. No heavy lifting needed. Take up to 7 days to complete your move.

→
3

We Pick Up

Leave the empty totes at your new place. We'll swing by within the agreed window and collect everything, so you can focus on settling in.

Why ToteMove?

We built the service we wish existed when we moved.

Dolly Always Included

Every rental comes with a heavy-duty dolly. No renting it separately, no back strain.

Flat-Rate Pricing

Pay one price: delivery, pickup, and the dolly are all included. No per-day charges hiding in the fine print.

Proudly Local

Based in North Brampton. When you book with us, you're supporting a local small business, not a national chain.

Eco-Friendly

Reusable plastic totes mean zero cardboard waste. Better for the environment, and sturdier for your stuff.

Uniform Stackable Totes

Standard-sized lids that actually snap shut. Stack safely in the truck, in the elevator, in your new home.

Flexible Scheduling

Plans change. We get it. Reach out and we'll work with you on timing, not against you.

Great for Renovations

Renovating? Totes beat cardboard every time. Protecting and moving your belongings room-by-room during a reno is the part nobody plans for. Heavy-duty plastic totes give you stacking, security, and reusability that cardboard simply can't match.

Home Staging

Decluttering for showings? Rent totes to quickly pack away everyday items and personal belongings while your home is on the market. Clean space, fast turnaround, easy to unpack when you sell.
