FAQ

Got
questions?

How many days before my move do you drop off? +

7 days before your move date — giving you a full week to pack before and a full week to unpack after.

How strong are the totes really? +

Heavy-duty plastic. They don't sag, they don't rip, and hold up to 60 lbs.

What sizes are the totes? +

24 × 20 × 12 inches, gray plastic, with attached flat lids. They stack like Tetris in your truck.

Do you deliver on weekends? +

Yes, we deliver and pick up 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. Weekend dates do fill up faster, so we recommend reserving yours early.

Do I need to be home for delivery or pickup? +

Not necessarily. Many customers leave instructions for a safe drop-off location. We coordinate with you in advance to make delivery and pickup as convenient as possible.

What's the difference between a moving tote and a moving bin? +

They're the same thing — we use the terms interchangeably. A moving tote or moving bin is a reusable, stackable plastic container used instead of cardboard boxes. Ours measure 24 × 20 × 12 inches and hold up to 60 lbs each.

Are plastic moving bins better than cardboard boxes? +

For most moves, yes. Plastic moving bins are stronger, waterproof, and uniform in size — making them easier to stack in a truck. They don't collapse under weight, nothing needs taping, and there's nothing to break down or recycle afterward. You also skip the 12 trips to Home Depot.

Are your totes the same as plastic moving boxes? +

Basically yes. Our totes are reusable plastic moving boxes — they hold the same things cardboard moving boxes do, but snap shut without tape, won't collapse under weight, and get reused 400+ times instead of heading to the recycling bin after one move.
