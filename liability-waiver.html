Liability Waiver

Last updated: October 2026

This Tote Rental Liability Waiver and Damage Agreement (Ontario) works together with the separate Rental Agreement — both documents apply to your rental. You review and electronically sign both during checkout, before payment, using the same name and address as your booking.

1. Acknowledgment of Rental

I, the undersigned renter ("Renter"), acknowledge that I am renting storage totes and any related equipment (collectively, the "Rental Equipment") from ToteMove for the rental period selected at checkout.

By electronically signing this Agreement, I confirm that I have read, understood, and voluntarily agree to all terms and conditions contained in this Agreement. I further acknowledge that this Agreement is governed by the laws of the Province of Ontario and the applicable laws of Canada.

2. Condition of Rental Equipment

The Renter acknowledges that the Rental Equipment is provided in clean, functional, and acceptable condition at the time of delivery or pickup.

The Renter agrees to inspect the Rental Equipment upon receipt and notify ToteMove as soon as reasonably possible of any visible damage, defects, or concerns. Failure to notify ToteMove within twenty-four (24) hours of receiving the Rental Equipment will be deemed acceptance of the Rental Equipment in satisfactory condition.

3. Responsibility for Rental Equipment

The Renter accepts full responsibility for the Rental Equipment from the time possession is taken until the Rental Equipment is returned to and accepted by ToteMove.

The Renter agrees to use reasonable care in the handling, storage, transportation, and use of the Rental Equipment and shall not intentionally damage, alter, misuse, or modify the Rental Equipment.

The Renter is responsible for ensuring that the Rental Equipment is used in a safe manner and in accordance with any instructions provided by ToteMove.

4. Damage, Loss, or Theft

The Renter accepts full responsibility for any damage to, loss of, destruction of, or theft of the Rental Equipment occurring during the rental period, regardless of cause, except where prohibited by applicable law.

The Renter agrees to promptly notify ToteMove of any damage, loss, or theft involving the Rental Equipment.

If any Rental Equipment is damaged beyond ordinary wear and tear, lost, stolen, destroyed, or not returned, the Renter agrees to pay the applicable repair, cleaning, replacement, or administrative charges assessed by ToteMove. The replacement or damage fee for each tote or item of Rental Equipment is $40. The Renter authorizes ToteMove to invoice the Renter for any applicable fees arising under this section.

5. Release of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by the laws of Ontario and Canada, the Renter releases, waives, and discharges ToteMove, its owners, directors, officers, employees, contractors, agents, successors, and assigns from any and all claims, demands, actions, causes of action, damages, losses, costs, or expenses arising from or related to:

  1. The use, handling, loading, unloading, storage, or transportation of the Rental Equipment;
  2. Any personal injury, illness, death, property damage, or property loss suffered by the Renter or any third party arising from the use of the Rental Equipment; and
  3. Any damage to personal property stored in, around, or transported with the Rental Equipment.

This release does not apply to liability that cannot legally be excluded under applicable Ontario or Canadian law.

6. Assumption of Risk

The Renter understands that moving, lifting, loading, unloading, stacking, transporting, and storing items using storage totes may involve risks, including the risk of personal injury, property damage, or loss.

The Renter voluntarily assumes all such risks associated with the possession, use, transportation, and storage of the Rental Equipment.

7. Indemnification

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Renter agrees to indemnify and hold harmless ToteMove, its owners, directors, officers, employees, contractors, and agents from and against any claims, liabilities, damages, losses, costs, or expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from:

  1. The Renter's use, misuse, possession, transportation, or storage of the Rental Equipment;
  2. Any breach of this Agreement by the Renter; or
  3. Any claim brought by a third party arising from the Renter's possession or use of the Rental Equipment.

8. Payment of Fees

The Renter agrees to pay all rental charges, late fees, cleaning fees, repair costs, replacement costs, and any other amounts owing under this Agreement within the time specified by ToteMove.

Failure to pay amounts owing may result in collection efforts and any remedies available under applicable law.

9. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the Province of Ontario and the applicable federal laws of Canada. Any dispute arising from or relating to this Agreement shall be brought before a court of competent jurisdiction in Ontario unless otherwise agreed by the parties.

10. Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable by a court of competent jurisdiction, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

11. Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the Rental Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Rental Equipment and supersedes any prior discussions, representations, or agreements relating to the subject matter of this Agreement.
