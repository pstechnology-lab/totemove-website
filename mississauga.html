HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. Zero cardboard.

01

We drop off

7 days before move day, your totes and dolly arrive at your door. No taping. No assembling. No trips to the hardware store.

02

You pack

Lid open. Stuff in. Lid shut. Stack like Tetris. Flat lids and same-size totes mean more fits in fewer truck trips.

03

We pick up

After your move, we collect every tote from your new place at a scheduled time. They go back into rotation — used 400+ times before retirement.

FAQ

Got
questions?

How many days before my move do you drop off?+

7 days before your move date — giving you a full week to pack before and a full week to unpack after.

How strong are the totes really?+

Heavy-duty plastic. They don't sag, they don't rip, and hold up to 60 lbs.

What sizes are the totes?+

24 × 20 × 12 inches, gray plastic, with attached flat lids. They stack like Tetris in your truck.

Do you deliver on weekends?+

Yes, we deliver and pick up 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays. Weekend dates do fill up faster, so we recommend reserving yours early.

Do I need to be home for delivery or pickup?+

Not necessarily. Many customers leave instructions for a safe drop-off location. We coordinate with you in advance to make delivery and pickup as convenient as possible.
